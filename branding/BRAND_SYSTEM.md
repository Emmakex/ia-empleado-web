# IA Empleado — Brand system

## 1. Brand idea

IA Empleado is not framed as a bot, prompt box or generic AI layer. The identity represents a **new type of workforce coordination** where people, specialized AI Employees and company systems operate as one governed team.

Primary phrase:

> Personas. IA. Sistemas. Un mismo equipo.

English:

> People. AI. Systems. One team.

The visual system should communicate **coordination, movement, trust and human continuity**.

## 2. Symbol

The master symbol is a circular system of three connected nodes. The three visible nodes represent:

- people;
- AI Employees;
- business systems.

The central negative/white point represents shared context and governed coordination.

The mark must remain recognizable at favicon size. Do not add text, tiny icons or extra orbit lines inside the master mark.

## 3. Humanized AI Employee

The AI Employee should feel like a digital colleague, not a toy robot.

Preferred traits:

- recognizably human face and posture;
- contemporary professional styling;
- subtle technological cues such as a halo, earpiece, luminous node, collar or orbit;
- approachable expression;
- no uncanny realism;
- no metallic full-body android unless a specific story requires it;
- no childish mascot proportions.

The master vector portrait in `public/branding/ia-employee-human.svg` is the reusable baseline representation for small UI surfaces, diagrams and animation prototypes. Higher-detail campaign art can evolve from the same visual grammar.

## 4. Color

### Core

- Ink: `#111827` — primary text, trust, authority.
- Primary Indigo: `#5B5FF5` — intelligence, coordination, active flow.
- Deep Indigo: `#4F46E5` — emphasis and high-contrast brand moments.
- Periwinkle: `#7C83FF` — glow, motion, secondary nodes.

### Surfaces

- White: `#FFFFFF` — primary surface.
- Mist: `#F8FAFF` — quiet premium background.
- Soft Indigo: `#EEF1FF` — AI context surface.
- Line: `#DDE3F3` — neutral structural borders.

### State

- Success Green: `#10B981` — approved, completed, healthy state only.
- Warning/Review: `#D97706` — human review or policy boundary when needed.
- Error: `#B42318` — confirmed error/destructive state.

Green must not become a decorative secondary brand color. Its semantic meaning is important.

## 5. Typography

Primary web direction: **Inter / modern system sans**.

Character:

- clean;
- confident;
- highly legible;
- compact enough for operational interfaces;
- expressive through scale and weight rather than ornamental fonts.

Headlines: tight tracking, strong weight, short lines.
Body: neutral, generous line-height, high contrast.
Eyebrows: uppercase or small caps with controlled tracking.

## 6. Shape language

Use:

- circles and orbital curves for coordination;
- rounded rectangles for systems, tasks and decisions;
- soft white cards with subtle elevation;
- thin indigo connection lines;
- diffuse mist/glow rather than neon borders.

Avoid:

- cyberpunk grids as the dominant language;
- heavy black interfaces;
- random particles with no workflow meaning;
- sharp gamer-style polygons;
- saturated rainbow gradients.

## 7. Motion language

Animation should explain work:

- a task enters;
- an AI Employee receives context;
- a system is consulted;
- a handoff occurs;
- a human approves when required;
- the result becomes complete and traceable.

Motion principles:

- slow ambient orbit for background coordination;
- short directional pulses for task transfer;
- green only when a step is actually completed/approved;
- no endless high-energy motion;
- `prefers-reduced-motion` must preserve meaning without animation.

## 8. Photography / campaign art

People should look like capable professionals, not stock-photo caricatures. AI Employees may be slightly more polished/illustrated than people but should belong to the same world.

Preferred composition:

- light or white environment;
- people and AI side by side rather than human vs machine;
- systems represented as tools/nodes around them;
- visible flow or handoff;
- strong negative space for headline placement.

## 9. Copy embedded in visual assets

Default to **no embedded copy** in reusable production assets. Keep text in HTML whenever possible for localization, accessibility and SEO.

Text-heavy campaign art may exist as a derived marketing asset, but must have separate ES and EN exports.

## 10. Brand hierarchy

```text
IA Empleado
  ├─ Empleado IA
  ├─ Equipo IA
  └─ Empresa aumentada por IA
```

The master brand mark stays constant. Product/category differentiation should happen through labels, diagrams and context, not by inventing a new logo for every role or team.
