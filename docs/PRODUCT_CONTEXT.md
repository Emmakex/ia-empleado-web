## Product definition

IA Empleado is a private, portable and capability-driven platform for creating, configuring, connecting and supervising AI-powered digital employees that execute bounded business workflows under explicit organizational policies and human oversight.

An AI Employee is not simply a chatbot with a job title. The product model is a governed execution unit with defined role, objectives, procedures, tools, permissions, knowledge/memory boundaries, approval rules, activity history and measurable outcomes.

The word “employee” is a product metaphor and does not by itself create an employment relationship.

## Three separate surfaces

```text
1. iaempleado.com — Commercial Website
   explain + rank + demonstrate + convert

2. Kairoseth Reference Lab — Demo Company
   governed workflows + synthetic data + proof assets

3. Customer Private Runtime — Production
   customer-selected AI Employees in approved private infrastructure
```

None is a mandatory runtime dependency of the others.

## Core commercial message

The website should explain early that:

1. IA Empleado creates digital workers for defined business jobs, not generic chatbots.
2. Each company gets only the capabilities it needs, integrated with its own systems.
3. Customer runtime can operate in customer-controlled infrastructure or a dedicated private environment.

### Important commercial restriction

Recruitment-selection decisions and regulated financial eligibility/risk decisions are classified as high-impact/regulatory profiles and must **not** be marketed as generic self-service activation products without explicit product/legal/risk approval.

## Reference capabilities by employee

### Customer Support

Typical public-safe themes:

- inbound email/chat;
- FAQ/knowledge answers;
- CRM/order/service lookup;
- case/ticket creation and updates;
- configured low-risk changes;
- escalation and human supervision.

Do not imply unrestricted refund/credit authority.

### Administrative

Typical public-safe themes:

- inbox processing;
- document classification;
- structured extraction/OCR;
- completeness checks;
- internal record preparation/update;
- forms, follow-up and routing;
- anomaly detection;
- human control for consequential actions.

### Accounting & Billing

Typical public-safe themes:

- invoice intake;
- OCR/document extraction;
- supplier/customer matching;
- duplicate detection;
- deterministic accounting-field checks;
- invoice draft/registration support;
- reminders and reconciliation assistance;
- reporting and approval preparation.

Do not imply unrestricted money movement or regulated credit/risk decisions.

### Sales / SDR

Typical public-safe themes:

- approved company/account research;
- CRM lead creation/enrichment;
- qualification support;
- personalized outreach drafting;
- approved sending/follow-up;
- reply classification;
- meeting booking;
- salesperson briefing.

Do not present invented external facts as authoritative or imply uncontrolled outbound activity.

## Optional capabilities are optional

Voice, OCR, vision, image generation, browser automation and local models are modular capabilities. Public copy must not imply every employee or customer deployment includes every modality.

## Private deployment message

Customer production can be deployed in customer-controlled infrastructure or a dedicated private environment. Customer data/secrets should remain within the selected deployment boundary except for explicitly approved external providers/integrations.

The public website must not become a dependency of normal customer workflows.

## EN/ES contract

English and Spanish are first-class prospect/customer languages. Public website content, SEO metadata, accessibility labels, media explanations and CTA journeys ship with EN/ES parity for released scope.

## Value and automation claims

Automation percentages in internal opportunity research are planning estimates, not contractual guarantees.

Actual automation depends on process standardization, data quality, integrations, exception rate, authority policy and customer environment.

Public metrics must distinguish measured, estimated, potential and verified values.

## Product status baseline

Baseline reviewed from the product repository on **2026-09-11**:

- Phase 0 — project foundation: complete.
- Phase 1 — portable capability-driven technical baseline: complete.
- Phase 2 — AI Employee core: in progress.
- Reference Employees and later commercial/runtime phases remain governed by the product roadmap and their explicit exit criteria.

Therefore public messaging may describe the product vision and catalog breadth, but implemented/demonstrable status must be labeled accurately and must not be inferred merely from roadmap presence.

## Truthfulness rule

Before publishing a material product claim, classify it as one of:

```text
IMPLEMENTED_AND_DEMONSTRABLE
AVAILABLE_BY_CUSTOMER_ADAPTATION
PLANNED_OR_ROADMAP
RESTRICTED_OR_REQUIRES_REVIEW
```

If the classification is uncertain, the claim is not ready for publication.
