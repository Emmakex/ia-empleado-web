## Ownership rationale

The process-automation landing owns the category/evaluation intent: what business process automation is, where AI fits, how rules and integrations combine, and what a controlled implementation looks like. The support guide owns the verb/how-to intent: how to choose the first process, score it, pilot it and expand only after measurable evidence.

The homepage remains the broad `agentes IA para empresas` / `IA para empresas` owner. It explains the product category and coordinated AI workforce. It should support the process-automation page but not compete for the exact process-automation category.

The Process Analyzer remains an interactive utility. It helps a visitor inspect a workflow but is not the canonical SEO owner for `automatización de procesos`. This keeps the tool useful without forcing it to compete with the commercial category landing.

## Changes

- Reposition existing ES/EN metadata, H1, lead and direct answer around researched category demand.
- Separate the category noun (`automatización de procesos`) from the implementation verb/how-to (`automatizar procesos con ia`).
- Preserve reciprocal landing ↔ guide relationships already provided by Growth content.
- Preserve explicit links from both surfaces to Process Analyzer as the interactive next step.
- Keep human approval and bounded permissions visible rather than implying full autonomy.
- Add browser coverage for ES/EN canonicals, optimized metadata/H1s, reciprocal links and Process Analyzer handoff.
